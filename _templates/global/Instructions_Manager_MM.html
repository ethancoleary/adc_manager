📋 General Instructions

Please read the general instructions below carefully.

For this study, you will act in the role of a Manager.

Your job is to select one worker from a group of four who all performed a task over multiple rounds. You will find out more about the task shortly. Your payment will depend on the workers' performances in a round.
Background
In December 2025, a group of Prolific participants (herein referred to as workers) participated in a competition. They individually completed a number of rounds of a maths-memory task. Each round lasted for 2 minutes. Their performance in this task was influenced by their maths skills.

In the next page, we will provide you with the instructions for the task and then you will have a chance to practise it. While you practise, you can familiarise yourself a bit with the task, but your performance will not be part of your final payment.
